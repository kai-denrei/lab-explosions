Research notebook

What this baseline gives us

Fuel, heat and soot evolve in a 3D fluid simulation. A volumetric renderer turns temperature into firelight, with procedural logs, smoke and sparks.

Listen for synthesized crackles and pops coupled to the fire. No image textures or recorded audio are loaded.

What to compare

Watch the bright roots, separated flame tongues, smoky tips and ember trails. Switch to night and thermal to judge which silhouettes survive without their original colour.

The thermal views remap visible brightness; they do not display simulated temperature directly.

Fit for Stalheart

This is a WebGPU research reference. It uses generated volume textures and a stateful solver; it does not implement the Three.js explosion-module contract.

Candidate transfers: flame brightness, soot timing, ember motion and sound coupling. Phone GPU cost and integration into the game remain unmeasured.
