Research notebook

    

      What this reference gives us
      
A four-cascade Tessendorf FFT sea with JONSWAP wind and swell, eikonal shore breakers, Jacobian foam, area-ratio caustics, planar reflection, Snell's-window underside and a volumetric underwater pass.

      
Floating props use GPU water queries for buoyancy, and leave ripple rings and splash crowns where they land. On the seabed off the lee shore, a Cutis-Maku colony hunts whatever comes near and a Nih-Dairia circles above it.

    

    

      What to compare
      
Watch glints, crest subsurface scatter and foam erosion under the four view filters. Night and thermal show which silhouettes survive without colour, as with the fire and the explosions.

      
The thermal views remap visible brightness; they do not display simulated temperature.

    

    

      Fit for Stalheart
      
A WebGL2 reference on three r186, not an importable r160 explosion module. Candidate transfers: splash crowns and Cauchy–Poisson impact rings for shell hits on water, spray billboards, and the foam flow map.

      
Phone GPU cost and integration into the game remain unmeasured here; upstream's phone numbers are in its README.
